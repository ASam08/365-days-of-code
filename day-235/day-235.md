#  A More Accurate Time Bar
Welcome to **day 235** of 365 days of code - coding every day for a year, little and often

Not much time today to play with this, but I did go take a look at what I did for the dashboard cards and applied it to the timebar, then tweaked it a little to get more accuracy. I ended up working out the exactly how long in ms (overkill maybe), and then setting the interval to that for each render. That does mean that it's only checking once every 5 minutes once the page has been open past the first change.

I also noticed that things were't quite lining up with the lines on the time column when it was at the 15m mark. It turns out I had added self-center to the timebar, thinking it would center it horizontally, but it was also centring vertically as well (or maybe only vertically). Anyway, removing that tidied that piece up pretty quickly.

I didn't get into writing any tests today, but I think I am happy with the time bar now, if I'm honest I thought that would be harder work than it was, I almost wonder why it took me so long.
