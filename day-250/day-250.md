#  Wardrobe App - New Beginnings
Welcome to **day 250** of 365 days of code - coding every day for a year, little and often

Day 250 and it's time for something a bit different. With everything I have done on Tempus so far, I have learned and learned and learned. There were choices made early on in the project that I have had to live with, or go back and fix, and now that Tempus is in a good place, it feels right to start a new project. Well...not new new...

A few years ago, when vibe-coding was becoming a thing, I had a go at it, to solve a specific problem. My wife wanted to plan outfits, but the only down time she really had to think about it was when our daughted was asleep, in our room, in front of the wardrobe. So I thought I could put together an app where she could take photos of her clothes, classify them by various attributes (colour, season, category etc.), and even put them together as an outfit.

Anyway, lovable did it's thing and I had an app, on Supabase, with a few edits, and a bit of googling, I basically had it where I wanted, everyone was happy.

Then I sort of just left it, life was busy, etc. There were some bugs that never got looked at, Supabase archived and then deleted the db (free tier), and now the app is *way* behind on package updates etc. So I'm resurrecting it, but starting from scratch, and of course writing it myself. I will use the old app code as references, especially for content etc. but the old one was a Vite app, of course I will be going with NextJs for this one.

I've developed out my architectural plan (never done that before), I'll be replicating alot of what I have Tempus running on, but I do have something different here, images. I have thought about it and done some looking, and in order to have a self-hosted, dockerized app, but ready for expansion, MinIO seems to be a no brainer for object storage for the images.

Anyway, after all that planning and thinking, it didn't leave that much time for code, I got the first initial build commits in, and I have the welcome page running. I also updated my newday powershell script for creating these daily notes, to add another template for this wardrobe app.

Phew! That's enough for today, that's for sure, more tomorrow!
