#  Email Change
Welcome to **day 237** of 365 days of code - coding every day for a year, little and often

Time to go back and tidy things up from an earlier piece of work. I added in the account update options (name, password), but at the time I left email disabled as it needed a bit more work than I was willing to put into it at the time, in order to get an MVP out.

So today I went back and started the work to enable email change. Mostly this was pretty straight forward, a bit of logic copy and paste from the name field, but because the data update flow is different, I separated email out to it's own tab on the account page.

I did have to do a little bit of work on the betterauth config, and I came to the point where I needed to decide whether or not to require email verification once the email had been changed. I don't currently have email verification enabled for the project so I went with no, but I will have a bit of a think about this as I may end up going back and enabling email verification for the whole app, it sort of seems like the right thing to do.

Anyway, it's all done and working, no tests yet (of course), more tomorrow!
