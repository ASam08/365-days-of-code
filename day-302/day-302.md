#  UI Tweaks
Welcome to **day 302** of 365 days of code - coding every day for a year, little and often

Not a massive one today, just some UI tweaks to the wardrobe grid. I didn't like how massive the images for the cards were coming out, and espcially with them only being thumbnails, they weren't exactly looking great, so I changed a few things up, made the grid 6 wide on larger screens, which shrunk down the cards and therefore the images. I also applied some interactivity with zooming the image in slightly while hovered, and made the image square as well.

Like I said, not groundbreaking, just little tweaks with the time I had today. More tomorrow!
